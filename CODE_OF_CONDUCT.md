# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tehseen Tech is committed to providing a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone who contributes to or uses this repository, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate with clarity and respect, assume good faith while investigating disagreements, welcome useful corrections, give credit for ideas and work, and keep feedback focused on the contribution rather than the person. When discussing agent behavior, security, or automation, explain risks and trade-offs without exposing private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption, impersonation, malicious code, credential exposure, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Publicity for this project must be relevant, transparent, and non-deceptive.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) when they involve safety, privacy, or abuse, or through the repository’s support route in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md) for general community concerns. Reports should include enough context to investigate and should not include unnecessary personal data.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or take other actions needed to protect the community and the project. Enforcement decisions will be guided by the commitment to safety, fairness, and constructive collaboration.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, examples, and project-related public spaces when a person is representing or materially participating in the Tehseen Tech Manus Skills community.
